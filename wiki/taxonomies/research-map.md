# Research Map

Domain taxonomy for the Cardiology Wiki. Trial pages will populate under each domain
once they are ingested via the pipeline at `/add-paper`.

## Domains

### Stable CAD
Revascularization vs. medical therapy in chronic coronary disease. Anchor trials:
COURAGE, BARI-2D, FAME, FAME-2, ORBITA, ISCHEMIA, REVIVED, ORBITA-2.

### ACS / STEMI
Acute management, timing of revascularization, completeness of revascularization. Anchor
trials: HORIZON-AMI, RIVAL, STREAM, COMPLETE, MATRIX, DANAMI-3-DEFER, FLOWER-MI.

### Imaging-Guided PCI
IVUS and OCT guidance for stent optimization. Anchor trials: ADAPT-DES, ULTIMATE,
RENOVATE-COMPLEX-PCI, ILUMIEN IV, OCTOBER.

### Physiology-Guided PCI
FFR, iFR, and resting indices for revascularization decisions. Anchor trials: DEFER, FAME,
FAME-2, DEFINE-FLAIR, iFR-SWEDEHEART, FAME-3.

### Antiplatelets / DAPT
Duration, de-escalation, and bleeding balance. Anchor trials: CURE, PLATO, TRITON-TIMI 38,
DAPT Study, TWILIGHT, TICO, MASTER DAPT, HOST-EXAM.

### AF + PCI
Anticoagulation and antiplatelet combinations. Anchor trials: WOEST, PIONEER AF-PCI,
RE-DUAL PCI, AUGUSTUS, ENTRUST-AF PCI.

### TAVR / Structural Heart
Transcatheter valve therapies. Anchor trials: PARTNER 1/2/3, Evolut Low Risk, NOTION,
COAPT, MITRA-FR, EARLY TAVR, DEDICATE.

### Lipid / Prevention
Lipid-lowering and cardiovascular prevention. Anchor trials: IMPROVE-IT, FOURIER,
ODYSSEY OUTCOMES, CLEAR Outcomes, REDUCE-IT, SELECT.

### Heart Failure
Pharmacotherapy and devices in heart failure. Anchor trials: PARADIGM-HF, DAPA-HF,
EMPEROR-Reduced, EMPEROR-Preserved, DELIVER, GALACTIC-HF, STRONG-HF.
