# DELIVER

## Clinical Question

In patients with heart failure and mildly reduced or preserved ejection fraction (LVEF >40%), does dapagliflozin reduce the composite of worsening heart failure or cardiovascular death?

## PICO

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Population** | 6,263 patients with HF and LVEF >40% (including patients with improved LVEF from previously reduced), elevated NT-proBNP, NYHA class II-IV, with or without diabetes |
| **Intervention** | Dapagliflozin 10 mg daily |
| **Comparator** | Placebo |
| **Outcome** | Primary: composite of worsening HF (hospitalization or urgent HF visit) or CV death |

## Result

Dapagliflozin significantly reduced the primary composite endpoint (16.4% vs 19.5%, HR 0.82, 95% CI 0.73-0.92, p<0.001) at a median follow-up of 2.3 years. Worsening HF was significantly reduced (HR 0.79, p=0.001), while CV death showed a non-significant trend (HR 0.88, p=0.13). Benefits were consistent across subgroups defined by LVEF (including the >=60% group), diabetes status, age, sex, and background therapy. Importantly, patients with improved LVEF (previously <40%, now >40%) also benefited, addressing a previously understudied population.

## What Changed?

DELIVER confirmed the class effect of SGLT2 inhibitors across the entire HF spectrum, complementing EMPEROR-Preserved with dapagliflozin data. Together with DAPA-HF, it established dapagliflozin as effective across all LVEF ranges. DELIVER's inclusion of patients with "improved LVEF" was novel and clinically important -- these patients remain at high risk and now have evidence-based therapy to continue. The combined DELIVER + DAPA-HF dataset provided the most comprehensive SGLT2 inhibitor evidence in HF.

## What Did Not Change?

- CV death was not significantly reduced, consistent with EMPEROR-Preserved.
- The benefit in the highest LVEF subgroup (>=60%) was consistent but with wider confidence intervals.
- It did NOT determine which HFpEF phenotypes respond best to SGLT2 inhibitors.
- No mortality benefit was demonstrated in HFpEF with either SGLT2 inhibitor.
- It did NOT address HFpEF with specific etiologies (amyloidosis, HCM) that may have different pathophysiology.
- Combination therapy with other emerging HFpEF drugs was not tested.

## How I Would Explain This to a Patient

"You have a type of heart failure where your heart's pumping function is normal or has improved with treatment, but your heart still does not work efficiently. A study of over 6,000 patients showed that dapagliflozin -- a once-daily pill -- reduces the chance of your heart failure worsening and needing hospitalization. This benefit was seen regardless of whether you have diabetes. We recommend adding this medication to your current heart failure regimen."

## Relevance for Indian Practice

- **Comprehensive SGLT2i evidence**: DELIVER, combined with DAPA-HF, EMPEROR-Reduced, and EMPEROR-Preserved, provides evidence for SGLT2 inhibitors across all HF phenotypes encountered in Indian practice.
- **Improved LVEF population**: Many Indian HFrEF patients show LVEF improvement on GDMT. DELIVER supports continuing SGLT2 inhibitors in these patients rather than de-escalating therapy.
- **Generic dapagliflozin**: Widely available and affordable in India (INR 5-15/day), making DELIVER findings immediately actionable.
- **Simplified prescribing**: Rather than tailoring SGLT2 inhibitor choice to LVEF, Indian practitioners can prescribe dapagliflozin (or empagliflozin) for any HF patient with confidence.
- **HFpEF recognition**: DELIVER data should motivate Indian centers to improve HFpEF diagnosis and management, which has historically received less attention than HFrEF.
