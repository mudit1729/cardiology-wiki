# AUGUSTUS

## Clinical Question

In patients with atrial fibrillation who have recently undergone PCI or had an ACS event, what is the optimal antithrombotic strategy comparing apixaban vs VKA and aspirin vs placebo in a 2x2 factorial design?

## PICO

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Population** | 4,614 patients with atrial fibrillation and recent ACS (within 14 days) or PCI (within 14 days) |
| **Intervention** | 2x2 factorial: (1) Apixaban 5 mg BID vs dose-adjusted warfarin (VKA); (2) Aspirin 81 mg vs placebo. All patients received a P2Y12 inhibitor (predominantly clopidogrel). |
| **Comparator** | VKA + P2Y12 inhibitor +/- aspirin vs Apixaban + P2Y12 inhibitor +/- aspirin |
| **Outcome** | Primary: ISTH major or clinically relevant non-major bleeding at 6 months |

## Result

Apixaban was superior to VKA for the primary bleeding endpoint (10.5% vs 14.7%, HR 0.69, 95% CI 0.58-0.81, p<0.001). Aspirin (added to the DOAC or VKA + P2Y12 backbone) significantly increased bleeding compared to placebo (16.1% vs 9.0%, HR 1.89, 95% CI 1.59-2.24, p<0.001) without a significant reduction in ischemic events. The combination of apixaban without aspirin (apixaban + P2Y12 inhibitor alone) had the lowest bleeding rate. Death or hospitalization was also lower with apixaban vs VKA and without aspirin vs with aspirin.

## What Changed?

AUGUSTUS was the largest and most rigorous trial addressing antithrombotic therapy in AF patients undergoing PCI. Its factorial design allowed two critical conclusions: (1) apixaban is safer than warfarin in this population, and (2) aspirin can and should be dropped early. This established the standard of care as a DOAC + single P2Y12 inhibitor (dual therapy without aspirin) after a brief period (0-7 days) of triple therapy. Guidelines were updated to recommend early aspirin cessation (within 1 week) in most AF+PCI patients.

## What Did Not Change?

- The trial was powered for bleeding, not ischemic events. Stent thrombosis was numerically higher in the no-aspirin group, though not statistically significant.
- It did NOT determine the optimal duration of P2Y12 inhibitor therapy (most patients were on 6 months).
- The trial predominantly used clopidogrel; data with ticagrelor or prasugrel were limited.
- It did NOT address patients with mechanical heart valves or severe mitral stenosis (these require VKA).
- The optimal timing of aspirin discontinuation (day 0 vs day 7 vs day 30) was not formally tested.

## How I Would Explain This to a Patient

"You have two conditions that need blood thinners -- an irregular heartbeat (atrial fibrillation) and a stent in your heart artery. In the past, you would have taken three blood thinners together, which caused a lot of bleeding. A large study showed that using two blood thinners -- apixaban for your heartbeat and clopidogrel for your stent -- without aspirin, is both safer and just as effective. This means fewer bleeding complications for you."

## Relevance for Indian Practice

- **VKA to DOAC transition**: Many Indian AF patients remain on warfarin due to cost. AUGUSTUS provides strong evidence to transition to apixaban when affordable, particularly after PCI.
- **Aspirin discontinuation culture**: Indian cardiologists often continue aspirin for prolonged periods. AUGUSTUS supports early discontinuation in AF+PCI patients.
- **DOAC cost**: Apixaban costs INR 30-50/day in India, compared to INR 2-5/day for warfarin. This is a significant barrier but offset by avoiding INR monitoring costs and bleeding complications.
- **Clopidogrel as default P2Y12**: Clopidogrel is the most common P2Y12 inhibitor in Indian AF+PCI patients, aligning with AUGUSTUS protocol.
- **Stroke risk**: AF-related stroke carries devastating consequences in India where acute stroke care is limited. Effective anticoagulation is critically important.
