# PIONEER AF-PCI

## Clinical Question

In patients with non-valvular AF who undergo PCI with stenting, do rivaroxaban-based antithrombotic strategies reduce clinically significant bleeding compared to standard triple therapy with a VKA?

## PICO

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Population** | 2,124 patients with non-valvular AF who underwent PCI with stenting |
| **Intervention** | Three arms: (1) Rivaroxaban 15 mg daily + P2Y12 inhibitor (dual therapy); (2) Rivaroxaban 2.5 mg BID + DAPT (modified triple therapy); (3) VKA + DAPT (standard triple therapy -- control) |
| **Comparator** | VKA + DAPT (standard triple therapy) |
| **Outcome** | Primary: clinically significant bleeding (TIMI major, minor, or requiring medical attention) at 12 months |

## Result

Both rivaroxaban strategies significantly reduced clinically significant bleeding compared to standard triple therapy. Rivaroxaban 15 mg + P2Y12 inhibitor (dual therapy): 16.8% vs 26.7% (HR 0.59, p<0.001). Rivaroxaban 2.5 mg + DAPT (modified triple): 18.0% vs 26.7% (HR 0.63, p<0.001). Ischemic events were similar across all three groups. The trial was not powered for ischemic efficacy and used a non-standard dose of rivaroxaban (15 mg daily, not the AF dose of 20 mg).

## What Changed?

PIONEER AF-PCI was the first randomized trial to demonstrate that a DOAC-based strategy could safely replace VKA-based triple therapy in AF+PCI patients. It established the proof of concept that eliminating VKA reduces bleeding, paving the way for AUGUSTUS, RE-DUAL PCI, and ENTRUST-AF PCI. It also introduced the dual therapy concept (DOAC + P2Y12 inhibitor without aspirin) to mainstream practice.

## What Did Not Change?

- The non-standard rivaroxaban dose (15 mg daily instead of 20 mg) raised questions about stroke prevention efficacy.
- The trial was NOT powered for ischemic outcomes -- safety of the reduced anticoagulation was not firmly established.
- It did NOT include a VKA + P2Y12 (dual therapy without DOAC) comparator.
- The three-arm design without a true 2x2 factorial limited the ability to isolate the effect of each component.
- Long-term efficacy data were lacking.

## How I Would Explain This to a Patient

"This was the first study to test whether newer blood thinners (instead of warfarin) could be used safely after stent placement in patients with atrial fibrillation. It showed that using rivaroxaban with just one other blood thinner causes significantly less bleeding than the old approach of warfarin with two other blood thinners. Later studies confirmed and refined this finding."

## Relevance for Indian Practice

- **Historical importance**: PIONEER AF-PCI initiated the paradigm shift away from VKA-based triple therapy, which was the standard in India.
- **Rivaroxaban availability**: Rivaroxaban is widely available in India and is commonly used for AF. The 15 mg dose used in the trial is the dose prescribed for AF patients with CrCl 15-50 mL/min.
- **Dose confusion**: The use of rivaroxaban 15 mg (not the standard 20 mg AF dose) creates confusion. Indian practitioners should note that AUGUSTUS (using standard-dose apixaban) provides more straightforward evidence.
- **Warfarin reality**: Many Indian AF patients remain on warfarin due to cost. PIONEER AF-PCI provides evidence to transition to DOAC-based strategies when possible.
- **Cost-effectiveness**: The cost of rivaroxaban (INR 25-40/day) must be weighed against the costs of INR monitoring and bleeding complications with warfarin.
