# ENTRUST-AF PCI

## Clinical Question

In patients with AF who have undergone PCI, is edoxaban-based dual therapy (edoxaban + P2Y12 inhibitor) non-inferior to VKA-based triple therapy (VKA + DAPT) for bleeding and ischemic events?

## PICO

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Population** | 1,506 patients with AF who underwent PCI within the previous 5 days |
| **Intervention** | Edoxaban 60 mg daily (or 30 mg if dose-reduction criteria met) + P2Y12 inhibitor (without aspirin) |
| **Comparator** | VKA (dose-adjusted to INR 2-3) + P2Y12 inhibitor + aspirin (100 mg) for 1-12 months |
| **Outcome** | Primary: composite of ISTH major bleeding or clinically relevant non-major bleeding at 12 months |

## Result

Edoxaban dual therapy was non-inferior to VKA triple therapy for the primary bleeding endpoint (17.0% vs 20.7%, HR 0.83, 95% CI 0.65-1.05, p=0.001 for non-inferiority). The point estimate favored edoxaban but did not reach superiority. The ischemic composite endpoint was similar between groups. ENTRUST-AF PCI was the smallest of the four AF+PCI trials and thus had limited power for superiority analyses or subgroup exploration.

## What Changed?

ENTRUST-AF PCI completed the evidence base across all four major DOACs (apixaban, rivaroxaban, dabigatran, edoxaban) for the AF+PCI population. It confirmed the non-inferiority of edoxaban-based dual therapy to VKA-based triple therapy, providing an option for patients already on edoxaban to continue their DOAC after PCI without switching to a different agent.

## What Did Not Change?

- The trial was the smallest of the four AF+PCI trials and only demonstrated non-inferiority, not superiority.
- It did NOT compare edoxaban to other DOACs head-to-head.
- Edoxaban is less commonly used globally than apixaban or rivaroxaban, limiting the practical impact.
- The once-daily dosing of edoxaban is an advantage for adherence but was not formally tested against twice-daily DOACs.
- Long-term outcomes and real-world effectiveness data remain limited.

## How I Would Explain This to a Patient

"If you are already taking edoxaban for your irregular heartbeat and you need a stent, this study shows that you can continue your edoxaban with just one additional blood thinner (clopidogrel) rather than switching to three blood thinners including warfarin. This approach is just as safe and effective, and simpler to manage."

## Relevance for Indian Practice

- **Edoxaban market share**: Edoxaban has a smaller market share in India compared to apixaban and rivaroxaban, limiting the direct applicability of this trial.
- **Once-daily advantage**: For patients who struggle with medication adherence, edoxaban's once-daily dosing may be advantageous.
- **Dose reduction option**: The 30 mg dose-reduction option for low body weight (<60 kg, common in India), renal impairment, or concomitant P-glycoprotein inhibitor use is practical.
- **Complete DOAC evidence**: ENTRUST-AF PCI ensures that regardless of which DOAC an Indian AF patient is prescribed, there is trial evidence supporting dual therapy after PCI.
- **Cost**: Edoxaban cost in India is comparable to other DOACs (INR 25-40/day).
