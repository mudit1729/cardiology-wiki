# RE-DUAL PCI

## Clinical Question

In patients with AF who undergo PCI, does dabigatran-based dual therapy (dabigatran + P2Y12 inhibitor, without aspirin) reduce bleeding compared to warfarin-based triple therapy (warfarin + DAPT)?

## PICO

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Population** | 2,725 patients with non-valvular AF who underwent PCI with stenting |
| **Intervention** | Dual therapy with dabigatran 110 mg BID or 150 mg BID + P2Y12 inhibitor (without aspirin, after 0-3 days of post-PCI triple therapy) |
| **Comparator** | Triple therapy with warfarin + aspirin + P2Y12 inhibitor |
| **Outcome** | Primary: ISTH major or clinically relevant non-major bleeding |

## Result

Both dabigatran dual therapy regimens significantly reduced bleeding compared to warfarin triple therapy. Dabigatran 110 mg dual therapy: 15.4% vs 26.9% (HR 0.52, p<0.001 for non-inferiority and superiority). Dabigatran 150 mg dual therapy: 20.2% vs 25.7% (p<0.001 for non-inferiority). The composite ischemic endpoint (thromboembolic events, death, or unplanned revascularization) was similar across groups. The 150 mg dose showed numerically lower ischemic events than the 110 mg dose.

## What Changed?

RE-DUAL PCI reinforced the dual therapy paradigm (DOAC + P2Y12 inhibitor, dropping aspirin) in AF+PCI patients and extended the evidence from PIONEER AF-PCI to dabigatran. It provided dose-specific data: dabigatran 150 mg BID offered better ischemic protection than 110 mg BID while still being safer than triple therapy. This informed dose selection in clinical practice, favoring the 150 mg dose for most patients unless bleeding risk is very high or age >=80.

## What Did Not Change?

- The trial used warfarin triple therapy as the comparator, not an active DOAC comparator.
- It did NOT include a DOAC triple therapy arm.
- Aspirin was used for only 0-3 days post-PCI, which is shorter than some operators prefer.
- It did NOT directly compare dabigatran to other DOACs (apixaban, rivaroxaban, edoxaban).
- The 110 mg dose is not available in the US (only in Europe, Japan, and India), limiting applicability in some settings.
- Long-term data beyond 14 months were limited.

## How I Would Explain This to a Patient

"If you have atrial fibrillation and also need a stent, we used to put you on three blood thinners at once, which caused a lot of bleeding. This study showed that using dabigatran with just one other blood thinner (clopidogrel, without aspirin) causes much less bleeding and protects you equally well from heart and brain problems. We will use the higher dose of dabigatran unless you have specific risk factors for bleeding."

## Relevance for Indian Practice

- **Dabigatran availability**: Dabigatran is available in India at both 110 mg and 150 mg doses, making RE-DUAL PCI directly applicable.
- **Cost**: Dabigatran costs INR 30-50/day in India, similar to other DOACs. Generic versions are becoming available.
- **Dose selection**: The 110 mg dose is commonly used in India due to bleeding concerns, but RE-DUAL PCI suggests the 150 mg dose is preferable for most patients under 80.
- **Reversal agent**: Idarucizumab (dabigatran reversal agent) is available at major Indian hospitals, providing a safety net for emergency situations.
- **Renal considerations**: Dabigatran is predominantly renally cleared; dose adjustment and avoidance in severe CKD (common in India) is essential.
