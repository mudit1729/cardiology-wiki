# ACC Scientific Session

## Overview

The American College of Cardiology (ACC) Annual Scientific Session is one of the most prestigious cardiology meetings globally, attracting 12,000-15,000 attendees. It is the primary venue for the release of ACC/AHA clinical practice guidelines and expert consensus decision pathways. The conference features late-breaking clinical trial presentations, guideline sessions, abstract presentations, and industry symposia. ACC has historically been the launch platform for practice-changing data in acute coronary syndromes, heart failure, preventive cardiology, and structural heart disease. The ACC also hosts the concurrent Cardiovascular Summit (i2 Summit) focused on interventional and structural heart innovation.

## 2026 Details

- **Dates**: March 28-30, 2026
- **Location**: New Orleans, Louisiana, USA
- **Format**: Hybrid with ACC.org providing virtual access and on-demand content
- **Key Themes**: Implementation of the 2025 ACS guideline, cardiometabolic risk reduction with GLP-1 RAs, TAVR long-term outcomes, health equity in cardiovascular care, and AI-driven risk prediction models
- **Registration**: Via acc.org; member pricing, trainee discounts, and international participant rates

## Historical Highlights

- 2025: Release of the unified 2025 ACC/AHA ACS guideline, merging STEMI and NSTEMI into a single document for the first time and endorsing P2Y12 de-escalation strategies.
- 2024: SELECT trial extended analysis showing sustained MACE reduction with semaglutide 2.4 mg in obese patients with established CVD, expanding the cardiometabolic therapeutics paradigm.
- 2023: Release of the 2023 ACC/AHA Chronic Coronary Disease guideline, introducing SGLT2 inhibitors and colchicine into the CCD management algorithm.
- 2022: Presentation of VALOR-HCM trial establishing mavacamten benefit in obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, leading to FDA approval discussions.
- 2019: AUGUSTUS trial presented at ACC demonstrating apixaban-based antithrombotic regimen superiority in AF patients undergoing PCI.

## India Relevance

ACC guidelines are widely referenced in Indian cardiology alongside ESC guidelines, making the ACC Scientific Session relevant for Indian practitioners. ACC/AHA risk calculators and guideline algorithms are commonly used in Indian clinical practice and board examinations. The ACC has an active International Chapter program, and Indian cardiologists are well-represented at ACC as presenters, moderators, and attendees. The i2 Summit within ACC is valuable for Indian interventional cardiologists. ACC.org provides extensive free educational content accessible from India. The annual FIT (Fellow-in-Training) programs are particularly useful for Indian cardiology fellows pursuing US fellowships or seeking to benchmark training standards.
