# India Live / ICFI (International Conference on Future of Interventional Cardiology)

## Overview

India Live is India's premier interventional cardiology conference, featuring live case demonstrations from top Indian and international operators. Founded by Dr. Ashok Seth and hosted at Fortis Escorts Heart Institute, New Delhi, the conference draws 3,000-4,000 attendees annually. It is distinguished by its emphasis on live case transmissions showcasing complex PCI, structural heart interventions, and peripheral procedures performed by world-class operators. The conference serves as a bridge between global best practices and practical Indian interventional cardiology, with dedicated sessions on cost-effective strategies and adapting Western guidelines to Indian patient populations.

## 2026 Details

- **Dates**: February 2026 (typically held in the third week of February)
- **Location**: Fortis Escorts Heart Institute, New Delhi, India
- **Format**: Hybrid (in-person with live streaming of cases and select sessions)
- **Key Themes**: AI-guided PCI, intravascular imaging standardization, CTO algorithm updates, TAVR expansion in India, drug-coated balloon applications, and South Asian-specific cardiovascular risk management
- **Registration**: Open to cardiologists, interventional fellows, and cardiac surgeons; early-bird discounts available

## Historical Highlights

- 2024: First live demonstration of AI-guided PCI planning with real-time lesion assessment at India Live, generating international attention for the integration of computational modeling in catheterization laboratory workflows.
- 2023: Landmark presentation of 5-year MyVal TAVR registry data showing non-inferior outcomes to imported valves, catalyzing wider adoption of Indian-manufactured devices.
- 2022: Successful live CTO PCI marathon with operators from Japan, Europe, and India collaborating on retrograde cases, demonstrating cross-continental technique sharing.
- 2019: Dr. Ashok Seth presented India's largest single-center radial access experience (>50,000 cases), reinforcing India's leadership in radial-first interventional practice.

## India Relevance

India Live is the most directly relevant conference for Indian interventional cardiologists. Live cases are performed on Indian patients with disease patterns typical of the subcontinent (diffuse disease, small vessels, younger age at presentation, high diabetes prevalence). Faculty combines Indian operators with global experts, creating practical knowledge transfer. Cost-effective strategies, generic device evaluation, and Indian registry data presentations make the content immediately applicable to daily practice. Networking opportunities with peers across India and mentorship for young interventionalists are additional strengths. CME credits are widely recognized.
