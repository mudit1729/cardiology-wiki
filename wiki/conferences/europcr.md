# EuroPCR

## Overview

EuroPCR is the world's leading interventional cardiology course, organized by the European Association of Percutaneous Cardiovascular Interventions (EAPCI) in collaboration with the PCR family. Held annually in Paris, it attracts 10,000-12,000 attendees from over 130 countries. EuroPCR is distinguished by its educational format combining live case demonstrations from leading European and global centers, interactive case reviews, hands-on device training, and late-breaking clinical trial presentations. The conference is structured around dedicated pathways (coronary, structural, peripheral) with content for operators at all career stages, from fellows to expert proctors.

## 2026 Details

- **Dates**: May 19-22, 2026

## Historical Highlights

- 2024: Late-breaking presentation of the FAVOR III Europe trial demonstrating that QFR-guided PCI improved clinical outcomes compared to angiography-guided PCI, reshaping the physiology-guided revascularization landscape.
- 2023: First live demonstration of autonomous robotic PCI at EuroPCR, with remote operator guidance via 5G connectivity, generating global media coverage.
- 2022: Presentation of the ILUMIEN IV trial (OCT-guided PCI vs. angiography-guided PCI) confirming minimum stent area improvement with OCT guidance.
- 2019: Live CTO PCI marathon featuring simultaneous cases from Tokyo, London, and New York, with real-time technical discussion and algorithm comparison.
- 2018: SYNTAX II presentation demonstrating that a contemporary PCI strategy (physiology + imaging-guided) could match CABG outcomes in complex multivessel disease.

## India Relevance

EuroPCR is a must-attend for Indian interventional cardiologists seeking to stay at the cutting edge. Several Indian operators are regular faculty (Dr. Ashok Seth, Dr. Tejas Patel, among others), and Indian case presentations feature prominently. The live case format is directly educational for technique refinement. Device and technology exposure at EuroPCR helps Indian operators evaluate options before they reach the Indian market. The Fellows Course is excellent for Indian DM cardiology trainees pursuing interventional careers. Travel and registration costs are the main barrier; institutional sponsorship or industry fellowship grants can help.
