# CSI / CSICON (Cardiological Society of India Annual Conference)

## Overview

CSICON is the annual scientific meeting of the Cardiological Society of India (CSI), the largest cardiology society in India with over 6,000 members. Attendance typically ranges from 4,000-6,000 delegates. Unlike subspecialty-focused meetings, CSICON covers the full breadth of cardiology: clinical cardiology, interventional, electrophysiology, heart failure, imaging, preventive cardiology, and pediatric cardiology. It serves as the national forum for Indian cardiology research, guideline adaptation for the Indian context, and professional networking. The conference rotates cities annually, with the local organizing chapter hosting in partnership with the national CSI body.

## 2026 Details

- **Dates**: December 2026 (traditionally held in the first or second week of December)
- **Location**: Rotating city (recent hosts include Kochi, Kolkata, Lucknow, Chennai)
- **Format**: In-person with select virtual sessions
- **Key Themes**: Adaptation of 2025 ACS guidelines for Indian practice, SGLT2 inhibitor and GLP-1 RA utilization across cardiology subspecialties, rheumatic heart disease burden, rural STEMI network expansion, and AI in cardiac imaging
- **Registration**: Open to all CSI members and invited international guests

## Historical Highlights

- 2024: Launch of the CSI-Kerala STEMI registry, demonstrating that regionalized STEMI care with hub-and-spoke model can achieve FMC-to-device times comparable to Western benchmarks in a developing-country setting.
- 2023: Landmark session on air pollution and cardiovascular disease featuring original Indian data linking PM2.5 exposure to ACS incidence, catalyzing policy advocacy by CSI.
- 2022: Release of CSI position statement on antiplatelet therapy in Indian patients, addressing the higher bleeding risk observed in South Asian populations.
- 2020: Virtual CSICON during COVID-19 attracted record online attendance and featured dedicated sessions on COVID-myocarditis and thrombotic complications.

## India Relevance

CSICON is the most important general cardiology meeting for Indian practitioners. It is the primary venue for Indian cardiovascular research presentations, networking with peers across all states, and continuing medical education credits recognized by MCI/NMC. CSI guidelines and position statements released at CSICON directly influence practice across India. The conference is affordable and accessible to early-career cardiologists and those from smaller cities. Exposure to the full breadth of cardiology (not just interventional) makes it valuable for general cardiologists who form the backbone of cardiac care delivery in India.
