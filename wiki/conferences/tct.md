# TCT (Transcatheter Cardiovascular Therapeutics)

## Overview

TCT is the annual scientific symposium of the Cardiovascular Research Foundation (CRF), directed by Dr. Gregg Stone and Dr. Martin Leon. It is the largest and most influential interventional cardiology conference in the world, drawing 8,000-10,000 attendees. Held in the United States (typically Washington D.C. or San Francisco), TCT is the premier venue for late-breaking clinical trial presentations in interventional cardiology and structural heart disease. Major device approvals, pivotal trial results, and practice-changing data frequently debut at TCT. The conference combines scientific sessions, live case demonstrations, hands-on device workshops, and the CRF Structural Heart & Valve Summit.

## 2026 Details

- **Dates**: October 2026 (typically late October)
- **Location**: Washington D.C., USA (alternates with other US cities)
- **Format**: Hybrid with robust virtual platform including on-demand content
- **Key Themes**: Next-generation TAVR durability data, transcatheter mitral and tricuspid therapies, coronary intravascular lithotripsy outcomes, AI-assisted PCI planning, and long-term data from short-DAPT and aspirin-free strategies
- **Registration**: Via tctmd.com; early registration discounts; trainee rates available

## Watchlist

- Coronary lithotripsy (Shockwave C2+) randomized outcomes in calcified lesions
- Left atrial appendage occlusion: Amulet vs. Watchman long-term comparison
- PCSK9 inhibitor and inclisiran cardiovascular outcome updates

## Historical Highlights

- 2024: Presentation of the AGENT IDE trial showing AI-assisted quantitative coronary angiography improved lesion assessment accuracy, marking the entry of machine learning into routine catheterization laboratory decision-making.
- 2023: CLASP IID pivotal trial results for transcatheter mitral valve replacement, demonstrating feasibility and safety in patients ineligible for surgical repair.
- 2022: DanGer Shock trial presented showing Impella CP improved survival in STEMI with cardiogenic shock, reshaping mechanical circulatory support practice.
- 2019: ISCHEMIA trial presented at simultaneous TCT/AHA, demonstrating no mortality benefit of routine invasive strategy in stable ischemic heart disease.
- 2017: ORBITA trial challenging the placebo-controlled benefit of PCI for stable angina, generating intense debate.

## India Relevance

TCT is essential for Indian interventional cardiologists involved in academic practice, clinical trials, and device evaluation. Late-breaking trial results presented at TCT directly influence global and Indian practice guidelines. The structural heart track is particularly valuable as TAVR, TMVR, and LAAO programs expand in India. Indian researchers and operators increasingly present at TCT, with growing representation in trial steering committees. TCTMD.com provides free access to all presentations, making the content accessible even to those who cannot attend in person. The time zone difference (US Eastern) makes live virtual attendance challenging from India but on-demand content addresses this.
