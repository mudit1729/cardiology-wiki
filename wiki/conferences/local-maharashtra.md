# Maharashtra / Pune Cardiology Meetings

## Overview

Maharashtra hosts a vibrant ecosystem of local and regional cardiology meetings, anchored by the CSI Maharashtra Chapter (CSI-MC) annual conference and complemented by city-level meetings in Pune, Mumbai, Nagpur, and Nashik. These meetings collectively draw 500-2,000 attendees per event and serve as the primary CME and networking platform for practicing cardiologists across the state. Unlike national or international conferences, these meetings emphasize practical clinical decision-making for the Indian patient population, affordable treatment strategies, case-based learning from local hospitals, and guideline implementation within the constraints of the Maharashtra healthcare system. The meetings are organized by local CSI chapters, hospital academic departments, and private cardiology groups.

## 2026 Details

- **Dates**: Multiple meetings throughout the year; CSI Maharashtra Chapter Conference typically in August-September; Pune Cardiology Society meetings held quarterly; Mumbai Cardiology Forum monthly
- **Location**: Pune (Symbiosis, hotel venues), Mumbai (Bombay Hospital, convention centers), Nagpur (Chitnavis Centre)
- **Format**: Predominantly in-person; some sessions live-streamed on YouTube or Zoom
- **Key Themes**: Implementation of 2025 ACS guideline in Maharashtra hospitals, STEMI network expansion beyond Pune and Mumbai, affordable GDMT for heart failure, managing rheumatic heart disease in Vidarbha and Marathwada, and practical interventional techniques for district hospitals
- **Registration**: Low-cost (INR 500-2000); often free for trainees and CSI members

## Watchlist

- Rheumatic heart disease screening programs in rural Maharashtra

## Historical Highlights

- 2024: Pune Cardiology Society launched the Pune STEMI Network connecting 12 spoke hospitals to 4 hub PCI centers, achieving a median FMC-to-device time of 135 minutes for transfer patients in the first year.
- 2023: CSI Maharashtra Chapter released a position paper on affordable heart failure management recommending generic quadruple therapy at a total cost under INR 100/day, widely adopted across state hospitals.
- 2022: Mumbai Cardiology Forum hosted a landmark session on air pollution and ACS, presenting data from KEM and Sion Hospital correlating PM2.5 spikes with STEMI admissions.
- 2021: Virtual Maharashtra cardiology meetings during COVID-19 reached cardiologists in Tier 3 cities who had never attended a state-level conference before, demonstrating the potential of hybrid formats.

## India Relevance

Maharashtra/Pune cardiology meetings are directly relevant to daily clinical practice for cardiologists in the state. The content is grounded in local realities: patient demographics, disease patterns, drug availability, cost constraints, and healthcare infrastructure. These meetings are the most effective platform for translating guideline recommendations into protocols applicable at the district and taluka hospital level. Networking with local peers facilitates referral pathways, mentorship, and collaborative care. CME credits from CSI-MC meetings are recognized by Maharashtra Medical Council. For cardiologists establishing practices in Pune, Mumbai, or other Maharashtra cities, these meetings are essential for integration into the local professional community. The low cost and accessibility make them the most democratic educational platform in Indian cardiology.
