# ESC Congress

## Overview

The European Society of Cardiology (ESC) Congress is the world's largest cardiology meeting, with 25,000-33,000 attendees from over 180 countries. Held annually in late August or early September, it covers every subspecialty of cardiovascular medicine. ESC Congress is the primary venue for the release of new ESC clinical practice guidelines, which are the most widely adopted cardiovascular guidelines globally (including in India). The congress features Hotline sessions with late-breaking clinical trials, guideline release sessions, symposia across all cardiovascular domains, and a massive industry exhibition. The sheer scale and breadth make it the definitive annual event in global cardiology.

## 2026 Details

- **Dates**: August 29 - September 1, 2026
- **Location**: Barcelona, Spain
- **Format**: Hybrid with ESC 365 virtual platform providing on-demand access
- **Key Themes**: New ESC guidelines (anticipated topics include cardiomyopathies update and pulmonary hypertension revision), heart failure paradigm evolution, AF management refinement, preventive cardiology and cardiometabolic disease, and digital health/AI in cardiology
- **Registration**: Via escardio.org; member discounts available; virtual-only option

## Watchlist

- New ESC guideline releases (traditionally 4-6 new or updated guidelines per congress)

## Historical Highlights

- 2024: Release of the 2024 ESC Atrial Fibrillation guidelines introducing the AF-CARE framework and CHA2DS2-VA score, fundamentally reshaping AF management globally.
- 2023: Hotline presentation of STEP-HFpEF trial showing semaglutide improved symptoms and functional capacity in obese HFpEF patients, opening a new therapeutic avenue.
- 2022: Release of ESC guidelines on cardio-oncology and ventricular arrhythmias; DELIVER trial confirming dapagliflozin benefit across the LVEF spectrum in HF.
- 2021: Release of ESC valvular heart disease and HF guidelines; EMPEROR-Preserved establishing SGLT2i benefit in HFpEF.
- 2019: DAPA-HF presentation establishing dapagliflozin as a pillar of HFrEF treatment regardless of diabetes status.

## India Relevance

ESC guidelines are the most commonly referenced clinical guidelines in Indian cardiology practice, making ESC Congress attendance (in-person or virtual) highly valuable. Indian cardiologists form one of the largest non-European delegations. The ESC 365 platform provides free or low-cost access to all congress content year-round. Indian researchers increasingly participate in ESC-endorsed multicenter trials. The ESC has active collaboration with CSI, including joint sessions and educational programs. For Indian practitioners, the guideline release sessions are the single most impactful reason to engage with ESC Congress, as these documents directly shape practice for the next 3-5 years.
