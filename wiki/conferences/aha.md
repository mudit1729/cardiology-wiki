# AHA Scientific Sessions

## Overview

The American Heart Association (AHA) Scientific Sessions is a landmark cardiovascular conference held annually in November in the United States, attracting 10,000-14,000 attendees. AHA Scientific Sessions is distinguished by its breadth, covering not only clinical cardiology but also basic and translational cardiovascular science, population health, nursing, and stroke. It has historically been the venue for some of the most impactful late-breaking clinical trials in cardiovascular medicine. The conference also has a strong focus on epidemiology, prevention, and public health, reflecting the AHA's mission beyond clinical practice.

## 2026 Details

- **Dates**: November 14-16, 2026
- **Location**: Chicago, Illinois, USA
- **Format**: Hybrid with extensive on-demand content via AHA Professional Heart Daily
- **Key Themes**: Cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic (CKM) syndrome as a unifying concept, advanced heart failure therapeutics, post-pandemic cardiovascular effects, obesity pharmacotherapy and CV outcomes, and precision medicine in cardiology
- **Registration**: Via professional.heart.org; member rates, trainee discounts, international attendee pricing

## Watchlist

- Late-breaking trials in heart failure, prevention, and cardiometabolic disease

## Historical Highlights

- 2024: Presentation of CKM syndrome framework as a new paradigm for understanding cardiovascular risk in the context of metabolic and renal dysfunction, with proposed staging from Stage 0-4.
- 2023: STEP-HFpEF and SELECT extended data confirming semaglutide benefits in obese heart failure and atherosclerotic populations.
- 2022: DELIVER trial establishing dapagliflozin benefit in HFmrEF/HFpEF (LVEF >40%), completing the evidence base for SGLT2 inhibitors across the heart failure spectrum.
- 2019: ISCHEMIA trial co-presented at AHA/TCT, demonstrating no mortality benefit of routine invasive strategy in stable ischemic heart disease with moderate-severe ischemia.
- 2014: PARADIGM-HF trial (sacubitril/valsartan) presented, transforming HFrEF treatment and establishing the ARNI class.

## India Relevance

AHA Scientific Sessions has significant relevance for Indian cardiologists, particularly those involved in heart failure, preventive cardiology, and epidemiology. The AHA's emphasis on population health and risk factor management aligns with India's massive burden of premature cardiovascular disease. The CKM syndrome framework is particularly applicable to India given the high prevalence of diabetes, CKD, and metabolic syndrome in the population. Indian researchers contribute substantially to AHA sessions through abstract presentations and clinical trial participation. AHA guidelines for resuscitation (BLS/ACLS) are the standard training framework used across Indian medical education. The AHA Professional Heart Daily platform provides free access to key presentations.
