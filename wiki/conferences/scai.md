# SCAI Scientific Sessions

## Overview

The Society for Cardiovascular Angiography and Interventions (SCAI) Scientific Sessions is the premier US-based conference dedicated exclusively to interventional cardiology. With 2,000-3,000 attendees, it is more focused than TCT but offers deep-dive content in coronary, structural, and peripheral interventions. SCAI sessions are known for practical, operator-focused content including live case demonstrations, complication management workshops, and device-specific training. SCAI also produces influential expert consensus statements and classification systems (such as the SCAI cardiogenic shock classification) that are widely adopted globally.

## 2026 Details

- **Dates**: May 2026 (typically held in the third week of May)
- **Location**: Rotating US city (recent locations include San Diego, Orlando, Phoenix)
- **Format**: In-person with select virtual streaming
- **Key Themes**: Cardiogenic shock management protocols, mechanical circulatory support algorithm updates, advanced CTO techniques, protected PCI strategies, coronary physiology integration, and operator radiation safety
- **Registration**: Via scai.org; member and non-member rates; fellow-in-training pricing

## Historical Highlights

- 2024: Updated SCAI cardiogenic shock classification incorporating biomarker thresholds and hemodynamic criteria for standardized staging, facilitating protocol-driven shock team activation.
- 2023: Expert consensus statement on minimum standards for intravascular imaging during PCI, proposing competency requirements for IVUS and OCT interpretation.
- 2022: Best practices document for protected PCI using mechanical circulatory support in high-risk patients, standardizing patient selection and MCS device choice.
- 2020: Rapid publication of SCAI position statement on cardiac catheterization during COVID-19, providing the framework adopted globally for prioritizing procedures during the pandemic.
- 2018: SCAI shock classification originally published, rapidly becoming the standard staging system for cardiogenic shock worldwide.

## India Relevance

SCAI is particularly relevant for Indian interventional cardiologists managing cardiogenic shock and complex high-risk PCI. The SCAI shock classification is widely taught and applied in Indian cardiac ICUs. The practical, operator-focused format translates well to Indian practice. Mechanical circulatory support (Impella, IABP, ECMO) availability is expanding in major Indian centers, and SCAI protocols guide their use. CTO and complication management workshops provide directly applicable technical skills. Indian representation at SCAI has been growing, with several Indian operators presenting complex case experiences. The relatively smaller conference size compared to TCT or ESC allows for better networking and mentorship opportunities with US-based interventional leaders.
