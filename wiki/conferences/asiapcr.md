# AsiaPCR / SingLIVE

## Overview

AsiaPCR/SingLIVE is the premier interventional cardiology conference in the Asia-Pacific region, organized jointly by the PCR (Percutaneous Cardiovascular Revascularization) family and the Singapore Heart Foundation. Held annually in Singapore, it draws 3,000-4,000 attendees from across Asia, the Middle East, and beyond. The conference follows the PCR educational model with live case demonstrations, interactive case reviews, and expert panel discussions, but with content specifically curated for Asian patient populations and practice settings. Asian coronary anatomy (smaller vessels, more diffuse disease, higher diabetes prevalence) and healthcare delivery contexts shape the educational agenda.

## 2026 Details

- **Dates**: January 2026 (typically third or fourth week of January)
- **Location**: Suntec Singapore Convention Centre, Singapore
- **Format**: Hybrid with live case streaming from Singapore and regional centers
- **Key Themes**: Complex PCI in Asian anatomy, TAVR in Asia (sizing, access, and outcomes), CTO PCI with Japanese technique emphasis, intravascular imaging as standard of care, and AI applications in interventional cardiology across Asia-Pacific
- **Registration**: Via asiapcr.com; regional pricing for Asian countries; trainee discounts available

## Historical Highlights

- 2024: Presentation of the Asian CTO registry showing comparable success rates between Japanese, Korean, and Indian CTO operators when standardized algorithms are applied, validating the transferability of the Japanese CTO approach.
- 2023: First Asian multi-center randomized data on drug-coated balloons vs. DES in small vessel disease, with Asian-specific vessel size criteria (<2.25 mm), showing non-inferiority of DCB.
- 2022: Launch of the Asia-Pacific TAVR consortium with standardized data collection across 12 countries, enabling the first large-scale analysis of TAVR outcomes in Asian anatomy.
- 2020: Virtual AsiaPCR during COVID-19 featured live cases with remote proctoring from international faculty, pioneering the tele-proctoring model that has since become standard.

## India Relevance

AsiaPCR/SingLIVE is highly relevant for Indian interventional cardiologists. Singapore's geographic proximity and affordable travel make it accessible. The conference addresses coronary anatomy and disease patterns similar to the Indian population (small vessels, diffuse disease, high diabetes burden). Indian operators are well-represented as faculty and presenters. The CTO workshops with Japanese technique training are directly applicable. Device and technology exposure includes Asian-manufactured products (including Indian stents and balloons) that are regionally available. The conference provides excellent networking opportunities with Asian interventional leaders. For Indian DM cardiology trainees, AsiaPCR is a practical alternative to European or US conferences, offering similar educational quality in a more accessible regional setting.
