# Atrial fibrillation

Atrial fibrillation is a supraventricular rhythm disorder with disorganized atrial activation and ineffective atrial contraction. In cardiology papers it often matters because it changes stroke risk, anticoagulation decisions, rhythm-control strategy, and procedural planning.

## Related Paper Themes
- Surgical or catheter ablation strategies.
- Anticoagulation and stroke prevention.
- Valve disease, especially rheumatic mitral valve disease.
- AF in patients undergoing PCI, when coronary intervention and antithrombotic strategy are part of the paper.
