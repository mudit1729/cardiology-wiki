# Rheumatic heart disease

Rheumatic heart disease is chronic valve damage after rheumatic fever. It commonly affects the mitral valve and remains an important cause of atrial fibrillation, stroke risk, heart failure, and valve surgery in low- and middle-income settings.

## Cardiology Relevance
- Rheumatic mitral stenosis and mixed mitral valve disease.
- Atrial fibrillation and anticoagulation decisions.
- Mitral valve surgery and concomitant surgical ablation.
- Applicability of trial evidence to India and other settings where rheumatic valve disease remains common.
